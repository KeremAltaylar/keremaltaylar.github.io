_Inside the Tribe_ places the listener inside a colony. The source material is drawn
from contact-microphone and low-amplitude recordings of ant activity — stridulation,
substrate vibration, the friction of bodies moving through tunnels — sounds that sit
almost entirely below the threshold of ordinary attention.

Diffused across an 8.1.4 array, these microsoundscapes are scaled up until the
listener's relationship to them inverts. What is inaudible at human scale becomes
architectural. The height channels are used to close the space overhead, so that the
audience is not observing the colony but enclosed by it.

## Notes

Premiered 10 May 2025 as part of the *Sonic Provocations* concert at Sonified III,
Arter, Istanbul.
