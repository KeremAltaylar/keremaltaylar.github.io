_Hammers of the Bruenor_ builds a composition out of struck and forged material —
metallic impacts organised into a rhythmic architecture that behaves less like a
groove and more like a mechanism.

The piece was developed alongside a written score and a structural diagram mapping
the relationship between its sections, and exists as both a fixed multichannel work
and a template for live performance.

## Notes

Presented at Sonified 2024, Istanbul.
