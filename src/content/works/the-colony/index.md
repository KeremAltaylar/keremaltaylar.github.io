_Colony_ is an ongoing project built around a self-made formicarium — an ant
habitat fitted with contact microphones and piezo pickups, designed so that the
colony's own movement becomes the sound source.

The work begins as field recording, at a scale where the field is a box on a
desk. It is a study in **microsoundscapes**: sonic environments whose events are
too quiet, too small or too slow to register as sound under normal listening
conditions, and which only become perceptible through amplification,
time-scaling and spatial diffusion. What the pickups return is not an ambience
but a texture of individual events — mandibles against acrylic, substrate
shifting, a body crossing a membrane.

The composition is made from those recordings rather than from the colony live.
Material is gathered over long stretches, then selected, stretched and layered
into a fixed multichannel piece, so that a rhythm which takes an hour to emerge
in the habitat can be heard as a phrase.

## Method

Custom 3D-printed module panels house the pickups against the habitat substrate.
Signal is routed into Max and Ableton for gain staging, spectral analysis and
processing, then composed and diffused across a multichannel array.
