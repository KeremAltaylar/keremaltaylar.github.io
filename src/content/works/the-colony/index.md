_Colony_ is an ongoing project built around a self-made formicarium — an ant
habitat fitted with contact microphones and piezo pickups, designed so that the
colony's own movement becomes the sound source.

The work sits between research and performance. As research, it is a study in
**microsoundscapes**: sonic environments whose events are too quiet, too small or
too slow to register as sound under normal listening conditions, and which only
become perceptible through amplification, time-scaling and spatial diffusion. As
performance, the formicarium is a live instrument — the colony is not a recording
to be played back but a system that behaves differently every time.

## Method

Custom 3D-printed module panels house the pickups against the habitat substrate.
Signal is routed into Max and Ableton for gain staging, spectral analysis and
real-time processing, then diffused across a multichannel array.
