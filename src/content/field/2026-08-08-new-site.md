This site is new. The previous one lived at `/Portfolio/` and was a single HTML file
with everything hardcoded inside it; this one is built from plain text files, so adding
a piece of work means writing a short document rather than editing code.

The immediate addition is the [Interactive](/interactive/) page, which collects
twenty-five browser-based pieces — five instruments and twenty generative systems —
that were previously scattered across separate repositories with no way in.

More works and field recordings to follow.
